\begin{abstract}
This work presents a machine-learning platform for predicting full cyclic voltammetry
(CV) current-voltage trajectories of ZnO-based supercapacitors. Six models are compared:
Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, a dense ANN, a stacked LSTM, and a stacked GRU.
A scientifically rigorous four-partition evaluation protocol is employed to test both
scan-rate interpolation and cross-material extrapolation.
\end{abstract}
